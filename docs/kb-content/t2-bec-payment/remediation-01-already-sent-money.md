# Response Card: I Already Sent Money

## Act in this order. Every minute matters.

---

**1. STOP all pending payments from the same request.**

**2. Report to your supervisor and company owner — right now.**
Call or walk over. Do not email.
Tell them: amount, destination bank, time sent, what communication you received.

**3. Call your bank's fraud line immediately.**
Use the number on your statement or official website — NOT from the payment email.
Say: "I need to initiate a wire recall / ACH reversal. The payment was fraudulent."
Provide: amount, destination account + routing number, date and time, reference number.

**4. Do not contact the destination account directly.**

**5. Preserve all evidence. Do not delete anything.**
- The original email with full headers
- The payment confirmation record
- Any attachments

**6. Call your cyber insurance carrier within 4 hours.**
Use your policy number. Ask about their fraud response process.

**7. File with IC3.gov within 24 hours.**
FBI Financial Fraud Kill Chain program. The faster you file, the more recovery options exist.

---

Money moves fast. Recovery windows close fast. Call your bank first.
