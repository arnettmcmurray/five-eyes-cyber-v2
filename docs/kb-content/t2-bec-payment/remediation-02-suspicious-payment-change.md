# Response Card: I Received a Suspicious Payment Change Request

## Do not act on the request yet. Do these steps first.

---

**1. Do not update payment records. Do not process the payment.**
Not even as a "test." Not even for a small amount.

**2. Check the sender's actual email address.**
Click on the sender name. Is the domain an exact match to the real company?
If no exact match → treat as fraudulent.

**3. Look up the vendor/carrier/person in your existing records.**
Find a phone number from prior correspondence, your TMS, or the company's official website.
Not from the email you received.

**4. Call that contact and verbally confirm:**
- Did they send this request?
- Are the new payment details correct?
- What is the reason for the change?

**5. Document the call: who, when, what was confirmed.**

**6. If dual approval is required, get the second approval before proceeding.**

**7. If you cannot verify, escalate to your supervisor before taking any action.**

---

A legitimate bank detail change can wait 30 minutes for a callback.
Only fraudulent requests cannot wait.
