# Lost or Stolen Device — Steps and Timeline

A missing phone with active load board sessions, saved TMS credentials, or freight email access is an open security incident — not a lost-and-found situation. The 4-hour reporting window is not arbitrary. Remote wipe commands require network connectivity on the device to execute. Every hour of delay is an hour the device remains a live threat.

---

## Step 1: Report Immediately — Within 4 Hours

Call IT or your designated device contact as soon as you realize the device is missing. Do not wait to see if it turns up. Do not wait until you get home.

- **Company-owned device:** IT will initiate a remote wipe command through MDM. The device will wipe when it next connects to any network.
- **BYOD with work profile enrolled:** IT will issue a remote wipe of the work profile and container only. Personal data outside the work profile is not affected. This is per the BYOD policy disclosed at enrollment.

If you cannot reach IT directly, escalate to your supervisor. The report must be made within 4 hours.

---

## Information to Have Ready When You Call

The faster IT can act, the smaller the exposure window. Have the following ready:

- Device make and model (e.g., iPhone 14, Samsung Galaxy S23)
- Which freight apps had active sessions or saved credentials: DAT, Truckstop, TMS, freight email, fuel card apps, factoring apps, any others
- Last known location and approximate time the device was in your possession
- Whether the device had a screen lock enabled (this affects how quickly unauthorized access is possible)
- Whether the device was on a cellular or Wi-Fi network when you last used it

---

## What Happens After You Report

Once IT receives the report:

1. IT issues a remote wipe command — the device wipes the next time it connects to any network (cellular or Wi-Fi)
2. IT will guide you through next steps: new device issuance for company devices, or BYOD re-enrollment instructions for personal devices
3. IT will assess which accounts were actively logged in and identify which passwords require immediate reset

Do not assume IT will handle the password resets automatically. Confirm with IT which accounts they are addressing and which you need to handle yourself.

---

## What You Should Do in Parallel

Do not wait for IT to finish their assessment before acting on accounts you know were exposed:

- **Change passwords immediately** for any freight app accounts that were logged in on the device. Start with DAT, Truckstop, TMS, and freight email — these carry the highest cargo theft risk.
- **Review account activity** on those accounts for unauthorized access: new logins from unrecognized locations, changed contact or payment information, posted or modified loads you didn't create.
- **Notify the finance team** if the device had access to payment routing systems, factoring accounts, or fuel card management. Payment instruction fraud moves quickly once account access is obtained.

---

## What Not to Do

- **Do not wait "to see if it turns up."** A device that has been lost for 24 hours and then recovered is a device that may have been accessed, cloned, or compromised during that window.
- **Do not assume the screen lock is enough protection.** Screen locks can be bypassed, especially on older OS versions or compromised devices. Remote wipe is the correct response regardless.
- **Do not attempt to locate the device yourself before reporting.** Use Find My (iOS) or Find My Device (Android) to confirm last location — but report to IT at the same time, not after.
- **Do not use "I'll handle it when I get back to the terminal."** Active load data, carrier contact information, and payment routing instructions have a very short window before they can be exploited.
