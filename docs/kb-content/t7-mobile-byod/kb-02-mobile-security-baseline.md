# Mobile Device Security Baseline

This policy applies to all company-owned mobile devices and to personal devices (BYOD) used to access company systems, freight applications, load boards, or dispatch communication platforms. Compliance is required as a condition of system access.

---

## Company-Owned Devices

All company-issued phones and tablets must meet the following requirements before deployment and for the duration of use:

**Enrollment.** Device must be enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform before it is issued to any employee. Unenrolled devices will not receive access credentials.

**OS updates.** Operating system updates must be applied within 30 days of release. Security-classified patches (as designated by the MDM platform or IT) must be applied within 72 hours.

**Screen lock.** Screen lock must be enabled with a minimum 6-digit PIN or biometric authentication. Auto-lock must engage at 5 minutes of inactivity or less.

**Encryption.** Device storage encryption must be enabled and confirmed by MDM before device issuance.

**Remote wipe.** IT must confirm remote wipe capability is active on the device before deployment. Employees must not disable remote wipe features.

**App sources.** Only apps from the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store may be installed. Sideloading (installing APK files or apps from outside official stores) is prohibited on company devices.

**Prohibited configurations.** Jailbroken or rooted devices will not be enrolled and must not be used for company business.

---

## BYOD — Personal Devices Used for Work

Employees who use personal devices to access company systems, load board accounts, freight email, or dispatch platforms are subject to the following requirements:

**Work profile or container.** Freight-related accounts and apps must be isolated in a separate work profile (Android) or managed via the company's container application. Personal and work data must not commingle in the same app environment where technically preventable.

**OS currency.** The device operating system must be within one major version of the current release. Devices running unsupported OS versions (e.g., iOS versions no longer receiving security updates) may not access company systems.

**Screen lock.** Screen lock must be enabled. This is a minimum requirement for BYOD enrollment.

**Remote wipe disclosure.** By enrolling a personal device, employees acknowledge that IT may remotely wipe work-related data from the device in the event of loss, theft, or separation. Personal data outside the work profile or container is not subject to remote wipe.

**Jailbroken/rooted devices.** Using jailbroken or rooted personal devices to access company systems is prohibited. These devices cannot receive security controls that company policy requires.

---

## All Devices — Company-Owned and BYOD

The following requirements apply regardless of device ownership:

- **Report lost or stolen devices within 4 hours.** Delayed reporting leaves freight credentials and active load data exposed. See the Lost/Stolen Device procedure.
- **No credential sharing.** Device PIN codes, app passwords, and biometric access are not to be shared with anyone, including co-workers.
- **No public USB charging stations.** Do not connect to unknown or public USB charging ports at truck stops, airports, or rest areas. Use AC adapters or your own power bank. This mitigates juice-jacking attacks.
- **Official app stores only.** Do not install apps from links sent via text, email, or messaging apps. Do not enable "Install Unknown Apps" settings on Android devices.

---

## Enforcement

Devices not meeting these requirements will have access revoked until compliance is confirmed. Repeat non-compliance is subject to disciplinary action under the company's acceptable use policy.
