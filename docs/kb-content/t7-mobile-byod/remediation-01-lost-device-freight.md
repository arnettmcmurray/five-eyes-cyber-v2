# Lost or Stolen Device — 5-Step Response for Freight Workers

A device with freight app credentials is an active security risk the moment it leaves your possession. These 5 steps limit that risk.

---

## Step 1 — Report to IT Immediately (within 4 hours)

Do not wait to see if the device turns up. Call IT or your designated device contact now.

**IT/Device contact:** ___________________________

Tell them:
- Device type and model
- Which freight apps had saved login credentials (DAT, TMS, Samsara/KeepTruckin, email, fuel card apps)
- Whether the device had a screen lock enabled
- Last time and place you had it

**Company device:** IT will initiate a remote wipe.
**BYOD device with work profile:** IT will wipe work data only (your personal data is protected).

---

## Step 2 — Change Passwords for Every Freight App That Was Logged In

Do not wait for IT to handle this. Do it now from another device.

Priority order:
1. Email account (broadest access)
2. Load board accounts (DAT, Truckstop, Convoy)
3. TMS login
4. Dispatch communication apps (WhatsApp business account if applicable)
5. Fuel card app
6. Any other freight-related accounts that were logged in

---

## Step 3 — Check Those Accounts for Unauthorized Activity

After changing passwords, review:
- DAT/Truckstop: any load postings or inquiries you didn't make
- Email: sent folder for messages you didn't send
- TMS: any record changes, new carrier entries, or load modifications
- Financial accounts: any transactions you didn't initiate

Report anything suspicious to your supervisor immediately.

---

## Step 4 — Notify Finance If Payment Systems Were Accessible

If the device had access to payment systems, factoring apps, or any account with payment instructions — notify your finance team so they can monitor for unauthorized payment change requests and alert customers if needed.

---

## Step 5 — Confirm Remote Wipe and Get Re-Enrolled

Follow up with IT to confirm the remote wipe was executed when the device next connected to a network. Get your replacement device or BYOD re-enrollment instructions.

---

## One-Line Summary

Report in 4 hours → Change all freight app passwords → Check for unauthorized activity → Alert finance if needed → Confirm wipe and re-enroll.
