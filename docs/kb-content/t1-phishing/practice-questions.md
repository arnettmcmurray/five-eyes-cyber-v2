# Practice Questions: Phishing and Email Security

All questions are scenario-based. Format: stem + 4 options. Correct answer marked *.

---

**Q1.** You receive an email from "DAT Freight Support <support@dat-freight.net>" saying your load board account is locked and you must verify within 2 hours or lose active loads. What is the safest next action?

A) Click the verification link in the email to preserve your loads.
B) Forward the email to your team so they know about the issue.
C) Delete the email immediately.
*D) Go directly to dat.com by typing it in your browser to check your account status.

---

**Q2.** You hover over a link in an email from "your TMS vendor" and the destination shows `https://login.tmssupport-verify.com/yourTMS`. What should you conclude?

A) The link is safe because it mentions your TMS vendor's name.
B) The link is safe because it uses HTTPS.
*C) The link is suspicious because the domain is `tmssupport-verify.com`, not your TMS vendor's actual domain.
D) The link is safe if the email display name matches your vendor.

*C) Ignore the link, contact dispatch on a known number to verify the alert, and report the text.
D) Forward the text to their supervisor.

**Q5.** A colleague forwards you a suspicious email that has a link claiming to offer a load board credential reset. What is wrong with this action?
