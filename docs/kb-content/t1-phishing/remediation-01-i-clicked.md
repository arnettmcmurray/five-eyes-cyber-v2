# Response Card: I Clicked a Link

## Do these steps in order. Do not wait.

---

**1. Stop — close the page. Do not enter anything.**

**2. Did anything download?**
- Yes → Disconnect from network now. Unplug ethernet. Turn off Wi-Fi. Airplane mode on phone. Do NOT power off the device.
- No download → Proceed to step 3.

**3. Report to your supervisor right now.**
Tell them: what you clicked, whether you entered anything, what device, what time.

**4. Change your password for any account the link was impersonating.**
Do it from a different, unaffected device if possible.

**5. Turn on MFA if it is not already active on that account.**

**6. Watch for warning signs in the next 48 hours:**
- Emails sent from your account you did not send
- Unexpected password reset notices
- Unusual login alerts
- Colleagues getting strange emails from you

**7. Report any warning signs immediately.**

---

Reporting is not optional — it is the most important step. Fast response limits damage. Silence lets it spread.
