# Practice Questions: Freight Identity, Verification, and Fraud Controls

All questions are scenario-based. Correct answer marked *.

---

**Q1.** A carrier bids on a high-value electronics load at a rate 20% below the market average. Their load board profile looks professional and their MC number comes up in FMCSA. What should a dispatcher do?

A) Confirm the carrier — they are FMCSA-registered.
*B) Apply additional scrutiny. A significantly below-market rate on a high-value load is a red flag. Check the FMCSA authority date, call the carrier at the FMCSA-listed number (not the load board contact), and consider supervisor review before confirming.
C) Accept the rate — legitimate carriers sometimes discount to build relationships.
D) Request insurance certificate verification before confirming.
