# Response Card: Suspicious Pickup Detected

## Multiple red flags on this load or pickup. Escalate before proceeding.

---

**1. Do not release the load.**

**2. Document the red flags you have observed.**
(Carrier mismatch, equipment mismatch, no pickup code, last-minute change, unknown contact, etc.)

**3. Call your operations manager or supervisor immediately.**
Do not handle this alone.

**4. Call the carrier at the verified number in your dispatch records.**
Not a number from an email you received today.
Ask: Are you aware of this load? Is this your driver and equipment?

**5. If the carrier is not aware of the load or does not recognize the driver:**
- Do not release. Treat as a theft attempt.
- Detain the driver professionally if safe to do so ("I need to verify — please wait here").
- Call your supervisor and law enforcement if needed.

**6. Preserve all evidence:**
- Driver information (name, CDL, plate numbers, any paperwork they presented)
- All communications related to this load (emails, texts, TMS notes)
- Do not delete anything.

**7. If you released the load before discovering the fraud:**
- Contact your supervisor immediately.
- Contact the shipper/customer.
- Contact law enforcement.
- Report to FBI via IC3.gov.

---

Trust the red flags. A suspicious pickup that turns out to be legitimate causes a short delay.
A stolen load is an operational and financial crisis.
