# Response Card: I Lost My Phone

---

**1. Report to supervisor and IT contact immediately.**
If company-issued: IT may be able to remote wipe it.

**2. Use your saved recovery codes to access accounts.**
Start with: email → TMS → load board → payment portals.
Recovery codes are stored in your password manager or your printed backup.

**3. If no recovery codes saved:**
Contact each service's support line to verify your identity and request access recovery.

**4. For each account recovered:**
- Remove the lost phone from MFA settings.
- Add your new phone.
- Generate new recovery codes.

**5. Review for unauthorized activity:**
- Recent logins from unfamiliar locations
- Sent messages you did not write
- Email forwarding rules that were added
- Any payment or load changes

**6. If you find unauthorized activity:**
Follow the "My account may be compromised" response card.

---

Recovery codes prevent this from being a crisis. Save them in your password manager.
