# Practice Questions: BEC and Payment Fraud

All questions are scenario-based. Correct answer marked *.

---

**Q1.** Your AP team receives an email from "FastPay Factoring <support@fastpay-billing.net>" announcing new ACH details for a carrier you regularly pay. What is the correct next step?

*C) Do not update the record. Call FastPay Factoring at the number on file to verify the request is legitimate.
D) Forward the email to your manager for approval before acting.
