# Got a Suspicious Text or WhatsApp About Freight? Do This First.

Freight-targeted smishing and WhatsApp fraud are increasing. The difference between a near-miss and a loss is what you do in the first 2 minutes.

---

## Red Flags That Mean Stop Immediately

- A text claiming your ELD shows a violation — with a link to "review" it
- A WhatsApp message from someone claiming to be a dispatcher, shipper, or broker you don't recognize — with a load offer, credential update request, or urgent payment instruction
- A text or WhatsApp asking you to update your load board login, TMS password, or banking details
- A QR code sent via text or found at a truck stop claiming to be related to a load, delivery, or compliance check
- Any load offer that arrives via text or WhatsApp from an unknown contact with a link to "confirm"

---

## Step 1 — Do Not Tap the Link or Scan the QR Code

If you already tapped it: go to Step 4.

---

## Step 2 — Do Not Provide Credentials or Payment Information

No one with a legitimate freight transaction needs your login credentials or payment account details via text or WhatsApp. No exceptions.

---

## Step 3 — Verify Through Official Channels

| If the message claimed to be from... | Verify by... |
|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| Your ELD/TMS vendor | Call the vendor's published support number — not any number in the text |
| A shipper or broker | Call the contact number on the original load confirmation — not the WhatsApp number |
| DAT or Truckstop | Go directly to dat.com or truckstop.com in your browser |
| Your dispatcher | Call them on the phone number you already have for them |

---

## Step 4 — If You Already Tapped the Link or Entered Anything

1. Do not enter anything else on the page
2. Close the browser
3. **Change the password** for whatever account the page was pretending to be
4. Enable MFA on that account if not already active
5. Tell your dispatcher or supervisor what happened

---

## Step 5 — Report the Suspicious Contact

Tell your dispatcher. Even if nothing happened, the intelligence helps protect other drivers and staff.

If you entered credentials: your supervisor needs to know so they can assess whether accounts were compromised.

---

## One-Line Summary

Don't tap → Don't provide credentials → Verify through official channels → If you tapped, change passwords immediately → Report.
