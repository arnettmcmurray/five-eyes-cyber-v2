# Response Card: Dock Refusal — Driver or Equipment Does Not Match

## The driver at your dock does not match the load appointment. Do not release the load.

---

**Script for dock staff to use with the arriving driver:**

*"I need to verify this pickup through our dispatch office before I can release this load. It should only take a few minutes. I'll need you to wait here."*

---

**1. Do not release the load. Stay calm and professional.**

**2. Note what the driver presented:**
- Driver name on CDL: ________________
- Tractor plate: ________________
- Trailer plate: ________________
- Any paperwork they showed: ________________
- Pickup code they provided (or stated they did not have one): ________________

**3. Call dispatch on the number in your records (not a number from the driver or a text you just received).**
Report: what does not match, what the driver presented.

**4. Wait for dispatch confirmation before any action.**
Do not release. Do not turn the driver away without supervisor direction — you need their information first.

**5. If dispatch confirms the driver is NOT authorized:**
- Inform the driver professionally that you cannot release the load.
- Do not return their paperwork — or take a photo of it first.
- Contact your supervisor.
- Document everything.

---

A delayed pickup you can explain. A stolen load you cannot recover.
