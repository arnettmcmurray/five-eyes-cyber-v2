# Do I Need to Report This? Incident Reporting Decision Guide

Use this card to determine who needs to know about a cyber or fraud incident, and how fast.

---

## First: Internal Reporting Always Comes First

Before anything external: tell your supervisor and IT/security contact. They determine what external reporting is required. You do not make external reporting decisions alone.

---

## Incident Type → Who to Notify → Timing

### BEC / Wire Fraud (payment sent to wrong account)
| Recipient | Timing | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Your bank's fraud line | **Immediately** | Fund recall window is hours, not days |
| Receiving bank's fraud line | Same call | FBI Financial Fraud Kill Chain requires both |
| FBI/IC3 (ic3.gov) | Within 72 hours | Builds sector intelligence; sometimes enables fund recovery |
| Cyber insurance carrier | Within 24 hours | Policy may require prompt notification |
| Legal | Same day | External communications clearance |

---

### Ransomware / System Encryption
| Recipient | Timing | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| IT/Security | Immediately | Isolation decision |
| Operations leadership | Within 15 min | Offline operations decision |
| Cyber insurance carrier | Within 24 hours | Coverage and IR firm engagement |
| FBI/IC3 | Within 48–72 hours | Required for most cyber insurance claims |
| CISA (TSA-covered entities) | Within 24 hours | SD 1580-21-01 v.C obligation for designated operators |

---

### Carrier Identity Theft (your MC/USDOT being used fraudulently)
| Recipient | Timing | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Operations leadership | Immediately | Scope the exposure |
| DAT / Truckstop security | Same day | Flag your MC as compromised on active load boards |
| FMCSA | Within 24–48 hours | Official complaint; FMCSA tracks these patterns |
| FBI/IC3 | Within 48 hours | Identity theft + wire fraud elements |

---

### Data Breach (customer/carrier data exposed)
| Recipient | Timing | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Legal | Immediately | State breach notification laws; CIRCIA (when finalized) |
| IT/Security | Immediately | Scope and contain |
| Regulatory counsel | Within 24 hours | NYDFS 72-hr obligation if applicable; SEC 4-day rule if publicly traded |

---

### Cargo Theft (cyber-enabled)
| Recipient | Timing | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Law enforcement (local + FBI) | Immediately | Recovery window |
| Cargo insurance carrier | Within policy timeframe (usually 24 hours) | Policy notification requirement |
| CargoNet / NMFTA | Within 48 hours | Feeds sector cargo theft intelligence |
| Operations leadership | Immediately | Load status and shipper notification |

---

## The Default Rule

**When in doubt: report internally.** Your supervisor and legal will determine what external notifications are required. Reporting too early costs nothing. Failing to report within a required window can create regulatory exposure.
