# Incident Discovered — The First 60 Minutes

Fast, correct action in the first hour limits damage and preserves evidence. Wrong actions — rebooting, deleting, staying silent — cost you both.

---

## Step 1 — Stop and Observe (0–5 min)

Before touching anything, write down:
- What you saw (describe exactly — error message, ransom note, login from unknown IP, wrong GPS location, unusual behavior)
- When you first noticed it (exact time if possible)
- What device and system
- What you had done immediately before

Take screenshots if you can. Paper notes if you can't.

---

## Step 2 — Do Not Reboot, Delete, or "Fix It" Yourself (immediate)

- Do NOT restart or shut down the affected system — this destroys volatile memory that investigators need
- Do NOT run an antivirus scan on the affected system without IT direction — it can alter or destroy evidence
- Do NOT delete suspicious emails or files — preserve everything

---

## Step 3 — Call IT or Your Security Contact Now (5–10 min)

| Scenario | First call |
|----------|-----------|
| Ransomware / encrypted files | IT/security immediately |
| BEC wire fraud / payment redirected | Bank fraud line first, then IT/security |
| Cargo theft with cyber component | Operations leadership + law enforcement |
| Carrier identity theft (your MC being used) | Operations leadership + load board security contacts |

## Step 4 — Alert Your Supervisor (10–15 min)

Your supervisor needs to know. They make the decision about external reporting — that is not your call to make alone.

Do not discuss the incident in group chats or with customers until leadership clears the messaging.

---

## Step 5 — Preserve Evidence in Place (15–30 min)

Do not move, rename, or open files related to the incident. If physical devices are involved (USB sticks, unfamiliar hardware plugged in), photograph them in place before removing anything.

---

## Step 6 — Await IT/Leadership Direction (30–60 min)

Your role is done after reporting. IT and leadership will direct:
- Whether to disconnect from the network
- Whether to activate offline operations
- Whether and when to contact external parties (law enforcement, insurance, regulators)

**Do not contact customers, partners, or press about the incident without explicit leadership approval.**

---

## One-Line Summary

Observe → Document → Call IT/Bank → Alert supervisor → Preserve evidence → Wait for direction.
